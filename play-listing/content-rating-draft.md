# Boba Buddies — IARC content rating questionnaire (draft answers)

Answered from the actual app (Boba Buddies mobile, v1.0.0). The game is a
virtual-pet caretaking game: feed, drink, bathe, play, sleep, and three
skill mini-games (Catch the Star, Memory Match, Jump!). There is no chat,
no user-generated content, no ads, and no user-to-user interaction of any
kind. All art and text are original and family-friendly.

## Questionnaire answers

**Violence**
- Does the game contain violent content? No.
- (Pets can throw a tantrum and show a grumpy face when neglected; there
  is no fighting, weapons, injury, or depiction of harm to any character.)

**Sexual content / nudity**
- Does the game contain sexual content or nudity? No.

**Language**
- Does the game contain profanity or crude language? No. All in-game
  text is family-friendly (e.g. care tips, pet blurbs, 'I love you!').

**Controlled substances**
- Does the game contain references to alcohol, tobacco, or drugs? No.
  (One pet, Boba, wears a boba-tea cup as a hat; boba tea is a
  non-alcoholic drink and is not depicted as a controlled substance.)

**Gambling**
- Does the game contain gambling, simulated gambling, or loot boxes? No.
  The three mini-games are skill games with fixed coin rewards; coins are
  earned by playing and spent in the shop on treats, toys, and room
  themes. Nothing is random-for-money and there are no loot boxes.

**User-generated content**
- Can users create, upload, or share content? No. There is no posting,
  no photo upload, no custom text, and no sharing feature.

**Chat / user interaction**
- Can users communicate with each other in the app? No. There is no
  chat, messaging, friend system, or any other user-to-user interaction.

**In-app purchases**
- Does the game offer in-app purchases? Yes.
  - Eight additional pets are one-time purchases, plus one 'Adopt All'
    bundle that unlocks all eight. No subscriptions.
  - Momo and Kiki are free; pets are bought with real money through
    Google Play Billing (verified via RevenueCat). In-game coins earned
    from mini-games buy only treats, toys, and room themes — coins never
    buy pets.

**Advertising**
- Does the game contain ads? No. No ad SDK is included.

**Personal information / data sharing**
- Does the game share the user's personal information with third
  parties? No. The app collects no personal information (see
  data-safety-draft.md). Purchase verification data goes to RevenueCat
  as a service provider only.

**Location sharing**
- Does the game share the user's location? No.

**Target age / audience**
- General audience — suitable for everyone (expected ESRB Everyone /
  PEGI 3). The game is designed for all ages; children can play it, and
  the app collects no personal information from anyone, including
  children. Purchases should be made by an adult or with an adult's
  permission (stated in the privacy policy).

## Notes for the submitter
- Complete the IARC questionnaire in Play Console under the store listing
  (Setup → Content rating). It generates the rating automatically; this
  draft is the input, not the rating itself.
- This app needs its own IARC questionnaire — do not reuse another app's
  rating. (Henledger has a separate Global Rating ID; it does not apply
  here.)
- If the questionnaire asks about 'digital purchases' or 'random items',
  answer: digital purchases — yes (pets); random items / loot boxes — no.
