## Full description (1,610 / 4,000 characters)

Your pocket is about to get a whole lot cuter. 🧋

Boba Buddies is a virtual pet game where ten adorable pals move right into your phone. Start with Momo, a sleepy mochi blob, and Kiki, a hyper little fox kit — both free. When you're ready for more company, eight more buddies are waiting to be adopted, from Boba the dreamy axolotl to Hana the sakura fawn.

Feed them, give them drinks, play together, tuck them in for naps, and keep them clean and healthy. Check in through the day — morning, lunch, and dinner — and your buddies will greet you with happy wiggles, excited cheers, and the occasional dramatic tantrum if you forget them for too long. (Sorry in advance. They hold grudges. Cute ones.)

Your pals grow up: egg, baby, child, teen, adult. Take great care of them and they may even grow into a sparkly adult form. Play three mini-games — Catch the Star, Memory Match, and Jump! — to earn coins, then spend them in the shop on treats, toys, and cozy room themes.

• Ten hand-drawn pals with their own personalities and moods
• Momo and Kiki are free — adopt eight more pals in the app
• Feed, drink, bathe, play, sleep, and care for your buddy
• Watch your pal grow from egg to adult (and maybe sparkle!)
• Three mini-games to earn coins for treats, toys & room themes
• Optional care reminders so you never miss a check-in
• Music and sound effects you can mute anytime

No ads. No accounts. No sign-up. Everything lives on your device and the game plays fully offline — your buddies are always there, even on airplane mode.

Adopt your first pal and say hello to Momo. He's been waiting for you. 💗
